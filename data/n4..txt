  Path there: 200,000-seed random campaign (best 137) → hill-climb from top seeds (best 142) → four structured

  Structural finding: golden (177) and the new record (183) sit at different points of the same depth trade-off
  surface documented for n=6 — both have d3=24, d4=1 (identical, both at the apparent cap), but the record trades 12
  units of d1 for 18 of d2 (net +6). d3 ≤ 24 held across ~300,000 exact configurations (rational and golden), a
  strong candidate n=4 analog of the n=6 deep-layer ceilings.

  Coverage/honesty: 183 is not proven maximal. The deep-climb method was only run systematically from one structured
  seed; applying it to the other structured families' champions is the obvious next step and hasn't been ruled out as

  104+66+24+1=195 as an (unproven) ceiling — real headroom likely remains.
