# `cube_regions_epsw` — a WIDE infinitesimal engine

## Why it is needed

[P166](../LEDGER.md) established that the 727 arrangement is the TANGENT structure
at the record: its chambers are infinitesimal directions, not regions of
configuration space. Evaluating them therefore requires the eps engine
(`cube_regions_eps`, METHODS 14), which computes over ℚ(√d)(ε) and returns the
ε → 0 limit exactly.

Measured with that engine, gated at `count_eps(pt, None) = 727`:

| stratum | eps-evaluable | median direction height |
|---|---|---|
| chambers | 17 of 25 (68%) | 215 |
| rank-13 faces | **0 of 20** | 3.5e5 |

The eps arithmetic costs ~2.2x in admissible component magnitude against q2's 512
cap — roughly 230. Chamber directions simplify below that; **deep-stratum
directions cannot**, because a rank-13 flat is a 2-plane cut by 13 equations and
contains no short rational vector. Those strata are where records sit
([P165](../LEDGER.md): rank 13 has 2 044 flats, rank 14 exactly one). Without a
wider eps engine the top of the lattice is unreachable.

## What to build

`cube_regions_epsw.cpp`, generated by `make_eps_engine_wide.py` from the
**validated** `cube_regions_q2w.cpp` (256-bit scalars) exactly as
`make_eps_engine.py` generates `cube_regions_eps.cpp` from `cube_regions_q2.cpp`.

**A generator, not a hand-edited copy** — so the derivation from a validated
engine stays re-runnable. Do not hand-edit the .cpp.

## State of the work

`make_eps_engine_wide.py` exists and already has:

- `SRC` → `cube_regions_q2w.cpp`, `DST` → `cube_regions_epsw.cpp`
- scalar-region start anchor → `struct FieldElem {\n    i256 p, q;`
- scalar-region end anchor → `static inline i256 gcdOfList`

It aborts cleanly (writing nothing) naming **7 unmatched anchors**. Remaining work:

1. **Re-base `NEW_SCALAR` on i256.** The generated eps scalar is a truncated
   polynomial whose coefficients are `Coef {i128 p, q}`. In the wide engine these
   must be i256, and every helper must use q2w's i256 API: `i256IsZero(x)` for
   `x == 0`, `i256ToStr(x)` for `std::to_string((long long)x)`, `iabs256` for
   `iabs128`. q2w's own `feToStr` (line ~1039) is the model.
2. **Adapt the 7 anchors**, both OLD and NEW sides. Five are mechanical
   i128→i256 / iabs128→iabs256 substitutions (`struct PKey`, `planeKey`,
   `gcdOfList({X.p,...})`, the `FieldElem(X.p/g, ...)` normalisation, the usage
   string). Two are structural: the magnitude scan (q2w line ~995 uses
   `i256 m256`, `iabs256`, `i256IsZero`) and `feToStr`.
3. **Re-derive the overflow budget.** METHODS 14 records that the eps convolution
   costs ~2592 overall, a factor ~2.2 in admissible magnitude. Recompute against
   q2w's budget rather than assuming the same factor, and insert it at the stages
   it arises, as `make_eps_engine.py` does.

## Gates — all required, none optional

The hazard is specific and stated in METHODS 14: if a truncation discards a
nonzero leading term, `feSign()` answers 0 for a nonzero quantity and the engine
returns **a wrong count, not a crash**. Assume nothing passes until measured.

1. `./cube_regions_epsw --selftest`
2. **Zero-eps reproduction.** With the eps part zero, `epsw` must reproduce
   `cube_regions_q2w` exactly on: `--d 0 --n 6` on the 727 record (727), the
   ℚ(√13) 727 configuration, and the two ℚ(√5)/ℚ(√2) 67s. Not "close" — equal.
3. **`eps_gate.py` adapted to the wide engine and passing**, including its
   decisive control: scaling a direction by 97 and by 1/1000 must not change the
   count. No finite-eps implementation can pass it.
4. `count_eps(pt, None) = 727` at the 727 record via the wide engine.
5. **The gain must be demonstrated**: re-run the 20 rank-13 directions from
   P166 Addendum 1 that the narrow engine refused. Report how many the wide engine
   evaluates. If the answer is 0, the engine is not the blocker and this spec is
   wrong about the cause — say so rather than adjusting the target.

## Deliverables

`make_eps_engine_wide.py`, `cube_regions_epsw.cpp`, the compiled binary, an
`epsw_gate.log` showing all five gates, and a postscript. Report unevaluable
counts as unevaluable; never score a refusal as a result.
